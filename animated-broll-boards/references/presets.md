# Visual Presets

Use presets only as loose art-direction hints. They are not themes, templates, or layout systems. The final scene must still be custom to the segment.

## `premium-saas`

Default for abstract ideas, metrics, dashboards, and neutral explainers.

- Dark ink background, crisp panels, electric cyan/lime accents.
- Large modern sans typography, restrained depth, thin grid/axis details.
- Best for: abstract explainers, fictional interfaces, metrics, comparison, process, risk, timelines.

## `construction-tech`

For building, logistics, manufacturing, architecture, prefab, energy, and field operations.

- Deep graphite background, safety-lime/blue/amber accents, blueprint grid lines.
- Use line paths, module blocks, site/factory labels, route nodes, blueprint transforms, material flow.
- Avoid literal clipart houses and repeated card stacks.

## `real-estate-premium`

For property, architecture, interiors, finance tied to homes, and buyer-facing content.

- Charcoal or warm off-white, brass/gold and muted green accents.
- More editorial spacing, elegant dividers, simple floorplan-like lines.
- Avoid beige domination and generic luxury clichés.

## `bold-reel`

For aggressive retention beats, warnings, myth/fact, and strong claims.

- High contrast, oversized labels, punchy color blocks, fast stagger.
- Use sparingly; it should feel intentional, not noisy.

## `minimal-editorial`

For calmer expert explainers and premium educational sections.

- Sparse layout, more negative space, thin lines, measured motion.
- Use when the narration already carries intensity.
