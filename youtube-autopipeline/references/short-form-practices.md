# Short-Form Reel Practices

Research inputs fetched during the May 5, 2026 pipeline update:

- YouTube Help: Shorts are vertical videos and uploads support up to 1080p; Shorts creation supports music, text-to-speech, effects, and preview before upload. URL: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en-CA
- TikTok Ads creative best practices: use a hook, unique selling points, and CTA; prioritize the hook in the first 6 seconds; introduce the content proposition in the first 3 seconds; use captions/text overlays; use transitions, stickers, and graphics. URL: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en&trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-text-block
- TikTok Creative Center guidance: keep important elements inside the safe zone, use vertical-native imagery, and make captions complete and legible. URL: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/tiktok_creative_accelerator/pc/en
- Meta newsroom: Instagram's Best Practices hub includes creation guidance on capturing attention, reel length, trending audio, and hashtags. URL: https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/

Concrete local rules:

- Open with a hook and visible content proposition in the first segment.
- For vertical reels, target `1080x1920` and prefer vertical-native source clips when fullscreen.
- Use `caption_text` on ordinary timeline entries for readable subtitle-style captions; use `TEXT` entries for short keyword hits.
- Keep captions away from the bottom PiP bubble and platform UI unless visual QA confirms the layout.
- Keep important faces, products, captions, and CTAs inside safe central zones.
- Use visual variety every few seconds: B-roll, PiP, stacked clips, text beats, local mock UI, stock footage, generated images, or presenter cutaways.
- Render variants when quality is weak. A completed MP4 is not deliverable until extracted frames have been inspected and recorded in `manifests/visual-qa.json`.
